      Mandatory constraints

        Optimization warnings

          Scientific objective coverage

          Agricultural viability is not confirmed by these instruments. It requires combined assessment of regolith chemistry, nutrient-related indicators, potential water indicators, and environmental conditions, with further investigation beyond this rover configuration.

          Team responsibilities

          Role assignments provide guidance only; they do not alter engineering values or calculations.

          Engineering decision notes

          Mass and payload

          Every installed system adds mass. Payload and mission limits can force trade-offs between instruments and the vehicle structure.

          Power reserve

          Systems draw from a finite nominal supply. A positive margin preserves headroom, but this model does not simulate changing sunlight or timed operations.

          Data return

          Science observations need a path back to mission control. Compare instrument data load with link capacity; relay windows and scheduling are not modeled.

          Cost and evidence

          A higher-cost instrument may add a distinct capability. Compare its science contribution and resource trade-offs; price alone does not guarantee mission success.

          Capability is not outcome

          A build can meet engineering limits and still fail to support a mission if its capabilities do not fit the site or objectives. Field operations are illustrative and do not produce real scientific outcomes.
